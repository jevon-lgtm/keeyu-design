Integration Model

How Keeyu Connects

Keeyu integrates with your existing systems through standard API connections. The Keeyu team handles the entire integration. You provide the credentials, and they do the rest. There is no custom code to write, no middleware to configure, and no changes to your existing infrastructure.

What You Need to Do

Provide read and write API access to the relevant systems. That is it. There is no SDK to install, no webhooks to manage on your side, and no ongoing technical maintenance. Keeyu runs as a standalone layer alongside your current stack.

How Long It Takes

Most brands are live within 5 to 10 working days from the point credentials are shared. The total effort on your side is two to three hours, covering everything from initial credential sharing through to go-live. There is no lengthy onboarding, no staging environment required, and no dependencies on your development roadmap.

Almost all of the work sits on the Keeyu side of the line.

Once Connected

What It Does

Keeyu monitors every order in real time across the full post-purchase lifecycle: payment, processing, fulfilment, shipping, delivery, and returns. When an issue is detected, a broken SLA, a lost shipment, a failed payment, the platform either resolves it automatically or guides the CX team through a structured resolution workflow. Every action is logged back into {{HELPDESK_NAME}} and the customer profile, so you have full visibility without switching systems.

What It Does Not Do

Keeyu does not replace any of your existing systems. It does not sit in front of the customer. It does not require any changes to checkout, storefront, or carrier setup. It layers on top of what is already in place and makes it work better.

In short

No code. No infrastructure. No dev time. No ongoing maintenance. Your involvement is limited to providing API access at the start, and that is the extent of it.

Month-to-month. 30-day exit. There is very little to commit to upfront and plenty of room to evaluate before making any longer-term decisions.
